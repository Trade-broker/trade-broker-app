Who We Are

BLACK GOLD NEXUS (PTY) LTD is a registered South African trade brokerage, incorporated with the Companies and Intellectual Property Commission (CIPC) on 17 July 2026. We operate as an intermediary, connecting buyers and suppliers and facilitating written negotiations between them — we do not take title to goods ourselves, and we do not touch inventory.

We're a newly incorporated company, and we say that plainly rather than dress it up. What we offer instead of years on the clock is a fully documented, verifiable structure from day one: registered with CIPC, registered with SARS, and every process below happens in writing you can keep.

What We Trade

Our current focus spans a small number of high-demand categories, and we work across others as opportunities arise. Every engagement is scoped to a specific category before outreach begins.

How a Deal Works

01

Enquiry & Research

We identify or receive a candidate buyer or supplier and verify what's publicly checkable about them before any contact is made.

02

Written First Contact

Outreach happens in writing only — email or text. No calls, no verbal-only commitments, ever.

03

NDA & Terms

Where relevant, a Non-Disclosure Agreement is signed before commercially sensitive details are shared, followed by a Commission Agreement setting out our fee.

04

Deal Execution

Buyer and supplier finalize terms directly, with documentation (sales contract, Incoterms, delivery terms) drafted to support the transaction.

05

Invoicing & Settlement

Our commission is invoiced only once the underlying deal is confirmed — never requested upfront.

How We Operate

All negotiation is conducted in writing — no calls, no video, nothing verbal-only
No profit guarantees are ever made, to any party, at any stage
Every commercial document (NDA, commission agreement, sales contract) is issued in writing before funds or goods move
We do not request upfront fees to "unlock" a deal — a standard advance-fee fraud red flag
Registration documents (CIPC certificate, SARS notice) are available on request for any active negotiation
